## 🚧 What the Curricula Actually Skip

Reading the public curricula above, the holes repeat across providers. Nobody we opened teaches:

1. **Measurement methodology.** How many times you re-run a prompt set, how you handle an answer that changes between runs, what counts as a baseline. Courses say "track your AI visibility" and then sell you a tracker.
2. **Mention versus citation.** A brand named in an answer and a brand cited with a link are different observations with different follow-ups. Only Ahrefs' materials engage with the difference properly.
3. **Off-page beyond 2019.** Skyscraper technique, broken links, guestographics - that is the link building being taught under an AI SEO title. Source ecosystems, comparison-page inclusion and data-led PR are where citation acquisition actually lives in 2026.
4. **Robot access.** Which AI crawlers you let in, what `llms.txt` does and does not do, reading log files for GPTBot and friends. Absent from every curriculum we read.
5. **Verticals.** Every course assumes B2B content marketing. Local, ecommerce and YMYL each get a sentence at most, and each behaves differently in AI answers.
6. **Runtime honesty.** Two of the paid listings do not publish total instruction time anywhere on the sales page. You cannot price-shop what you cannot measure.
7. **What to do when you rank but do not get cited.** Passage extraction, corroboration, restructuring. The closest any course gets is one CXL module on comparison content.

## 🧭 AI SEO vs SEO vs GEO vs AEO

The terms overlap. Do not turn terminology into theology.

| Term | Full Name | What It Means |
|------|-----------|---------------|
| **SEO** | Search Engine Optimisation | Classic organic visibility: technical SEO, crawlability, indexability, query targeting, content, internal links, backlinks, authority, intent, conversion. |
| **AI SEO** | - | Umbrella term for optimisation where classic results and AI discovery coexist: SEO plus mentions, citations, source selection, entities, retrieval, third-party corroboration and AI visibility measurement. |
| **GEO** | Generative Engine Optimisation | Improving the odds a brand, site or source is retrieved and cited by generative systems (AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity). |
| **AEO** | Answer Engine Optimisation | Making information suitable for direct answer experiences. Descends from the featured-snippet era. |

The useful question is always: what visibility problem are you solving, and what evidence will tell you the intervention worked? Judge curricula by substance, not labels.

## 🧩 The 8-Module AI SEO Curriculum

A practical framework for organising AI SEO education. A full implementation is free at **[AISEOCourse.academy](https://aiseocourse.academy/)**.

### Module 1: SEO Fundamentals

AI SEO is not an excuse to ignore SEO. Before optimising for AI-generated answers, understand search intent, crawling, rendering, indexability, canonicalisation, site architecture, internal linking, on-page optimisation, SERP analysis, backlinks, authority, content quality, commercial intent and conversion.

Minimum competency check - you should be able to answer:

- Can search engines crawl the important URL, and is it indexable?
- What query and intent is the page solving, and what page types dominate that SERP?
- How is authority flowing internally, and what external authority supports the page?
- What action should the visitor take?

If those questions are unclear, AI SEO tactics get built on sand.

### Module 2: How AI Search Works

You do not need to become an ML researcher. You need a working model of the difference between traditional ranked results, generated answers, cited sources, mentioned brands, retrieval, source eligibility and answer variability.

- **Retrieval** - a system must locate relevant information before it can use it in an answer. Clear, specific, retrievable information is therefore valuable.
- **Citation** - a displayed source reference attached to an AI-generated answer. A mention and a citation are not the same thing.
- **Query fan-out** - AI search can issue related searches across subtopics. The visible query may not be the only query involved in assembling the answer.
- **Ranking vs citation** - a URL can rank without being cited, and a source can influence an answer without holding the top blue link. Measure them separately.

### Module 3: AI SEO and GEO Research

Do not start with content production. Start with the query universe and the source landscape.

Build a query universe covering multiple intent types: informational (what is AI SEO?), commercial (best AI SEO course), comparison (GEO vs AEO), recommendation (AI SEO training for beginners), alternatives, brand, competitor and trust validation (is [brand] legitimate? who runs [brand]?).

For each query, record date and time, platform, organic position, whether an AI answer appeared, whether your brand was mentioned, competitors mentioned, cited domains, cited URLs, source types, context and notes. One prompt run once is not a baseline.

### Module 4: Content and Retrieval

Commodity AI writing is cheap. Information that is clear, specific, evidenced and easy to retrieve is valuable.

Strong passages carry a descriptive heading, an explicit entity, a direct answer, enough context to stand alone, evidence where needed, and no throat-clearing.

> ❌ *Weak:* "In today's rapidly evolving digital landscape, businesses need to stay ahead of the curve and embrace innovative strategies."
>
> ✅ *Better:* "An AI SEO course teaches search optimisation for both traditional search results and AI-generated search experiences. Modern curricula add GEO, citation analysis, entity optimisation, retrieval and AI visibility measurement to conventional SEO fundamentals."

Useful formats: definitions, comparison tables, checklists, ordered workflows, datasets, FAQs, original experiments, first-hand evidence, downloadable templates. Pick the format that makes the information easier to understand. A table added because tables look "AI friendly" helps nobody.

### Module 5: Entities and Brand Authority

AI-search visibility involves more than one URL. Systems may need to understand relationships between your company, founder, authors, products, services, locations, topics and external references.

Check consistency across: official brand name, domain, organisation description, leadership, product names, location information, social profiles, author profiles, structured data, third-party profiles, directories and media mentions.

Structured data clarifies information. It does not manufacture authority. Third-party corroboration does the heavy lifting.

### Module 6: Citations and Off-Page AI SEO

Link building still matters, but AI visibility widens the off-page problem. Map which third-party domains appear repeatedly for your query set, which publications get cited, which communities surface, which comparison sites and directories get used, and which competitor pages keep getting referenced.

Source types worth inspecting: specialist publications, industry blogs, news media, comparison sites, directories, educational resources, communities, forums, review platforms, documentation and research papers.

The goal is not "get mentioned everywhere". It is: become present in the source ecosystem that matters for the commercially important query set.

### Module 7: AI Visibility Measurement

Track four layers, because rankings alone no longer describe visibility:

| Layer | Metrics |
|-------|---------|
| Traditional search | rankings, impressions, clicks, organic sessions, conversions |
| AI search | brand mentions, answer presence, citations, cited URLs and domains, source share, competitor mentions, context |
| Entity / brand | branded search, knowledge consistency, third-party mentions, review signals |
| Commercial | leads, qualified leads, revenue, assisted conversions, demo/signup actions |

Visibility is an input. Business outcomes are the output.

### Module 8: Testing and Scaling

AI SEO is too young for doctrine. Run a loop: research, baseline, hypothesis, change, observe, compare, scale or stop.

> *Example hypothesis:* "Pages with concise answer passages and stronger third-party corroboration will gain more citation visibility across our fixed commercial query set."

Define queries, platforms, observation period, pages, intervention, success threshold and kill criteria before you touch anything. Otherwise every outcome can be rationalised after the fact.

## 🧰 Workflows, Templates and Checklists

### 🔄 AI Citation Analysis Workflow

1. Build a repeatable query set.
2. Segment queries by intent.
3. Run the same queries on the selected platforms.
4. Record brands mentioned.
5. Record cited domains.
6. Record cited URLs.
7. Classify source type.
8. Identify recurring domains.
9. Compare competitor visibility.
10. Map gaps to owned-content opportunities.
11. Map gaps to third-party-source opportunities.
12. Improve retrievability where appropriate.
13. Acquire legitimate relevant coverage where appropriate.
14. Repeat the same query set.
15. Compare changes.

## 🧐 How to Evaluate an AI SEO Course

Seven questions. The order matters.

**1. Is it actually about AI search?** Read the curriculum, not the title. AI Overviews, AI Mode, GEO/AEO, retrieval, citations, entities, measurement - or a classic SEO course with "AI" bolted onto the sales page? The URL often remembers what the title changed from.

**2. Does it preserve SEO fundamentals?** A course teaching AI tricks while ignoring crawling, indexing, architecture, links and intent is incomplete in both halves.

**3. Is the material current?** Check the last-updated date, the platform names, the screenshots. Course copy that references last year - or the wrong platform - tells you the update cadence for free.

**4. Is there evidence behind the teaching?** Public tests, datasets, case studies, reproducible workflows, documented methodology. Confident presentation is not evidence.

**5. Can you get feedback?** Live calls, cohort Q&A, community, office hours. Matters more for advanced practitioners; if you only need fundamentals, do not pay a premium for access.

**6. What exactly are you paying for?** Lessons, instructor access, community, tools, templates, certification, updates. A 20-hour course is not automatically better than a 90-minute one - check our [cost per hour table](#-cost-per-hour-of-instruction) before assuming price signals depth.

**7. Can the claims be checked?** Student counts, review scores, revenue claims, module counts. If a number has no source, treat it as unverified rather than filling the gap with trust.

## 🆚 Free vs Paid Training

| Free Training | Paid Training |
|---------------|---------------|
| Excellent for fundamentals | Can add feedback and accountability |
| Easy to sample several viewpoints | May provide current operator access |
| Often vendor-supported | May be independent, practitioner-led |
| Can be technically deep | Cohorts can answer your specific questions |
| No financial risk | Recurring communities add up quietly |
| Requires self-direction | Structure can accelerate implementation |

**Start free when:** you are new, you have no project to practise on, the terminology is still confusing, or you have not finished the good free material yet.

**Consider paid when:** you are implementing on real sites, the cost of mistakes exceeds the course cost, you need feedback, or you have a specific bottleneck a product actually solves.

Spend two weeks in the free material before giving anyone your card. If you still hit a wall on your own sites, pay - and by then you will know which room is worth paying for.

## 🚨 Common AI SEO Course Red Flags

Some of these we found on page one of Google this week, which should tell you something:

- The course title says AI SEO and the URL says "get to number 1 in Google search"
- The curriculum is keyword research, meta titles and Core Web Vitals with one AI section
- The course copy promises a certificate from a different platform than the one selling it
- A roundup lists the publisher's own products at #1 and closes by recommending itself - sometimes in a table of contents that cannot count to seven
- The runtime is not published anywhere on the sales page
- The provider implies guaranteed citations in AI answers
- Case studies cannot be inspected, or testimonials are anonymous
- Affiliate relationships are hidden
- Prices are quoted with no verification date, or are permanently "84% off"
- "Proprietary research" with no methodology
- A single universal "GEO score" computed from one run of five prompts
- The course teaches tactics with no measurement, or measurement with no business outcomes

## 📖 Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **AI SEO** | Broad term for search optimisation across conventional search and AI-powered discovery. |
| **GEO** | Generative Engine Optimisation - work intended to improve visibility in generative search and answer experiences. |
| **AEO** | Answer Engine Optimisation - optimisation for answer-oriented search and discovery. |
| **AI Overview** | Google's AI-generated search-result experience presenting generated summaries and links. |
| **AI Mode** | Google's conversational AI-search experience. |
| **Citation** | A source reference shown with an AI-generated answer. |
| **Mention** | A brand or entity appearing in an answer; not necessarily a citation. |
| **Retrieval** | The process of locating relevant information for use by a search or answer system. |
| **Entity** | A distinct thing: person, company, product, service, location. |
| **Query fan-out** | The use of related searches and subqueries to explore multiple aspects of a question. |
| **Information gain** | Useful new information beyond what competing documents already repeat. |
| **Source share** | How often a domain appears across a defined query set relative to other sources. |
| **SERP** | Search Engine Results Page. |
| **Structured data** | Machine-readable markup describing page entities and content; should match visible content. |

## ❓ FAQ

**What is the best AI SEO course?**
There is no single best for every learner. Choose by skill level, budget, need for live feedback, technical depth and whether you need fundamentals or implementation support. Free structured start: [AISEOCourse.academy](https://aiseocourse.academy/). Reference framework: [AISEOCourse.co](https://aiseocourse.co/). Paid practitioner community: [AI SEO Rainmakers](https://www.skool.com/ai-seo-rainmakers/about?ref=664c257fd6a84a3b94a2cb2a616d053d) *(affiliate link)*.

**Is there a free AI SEO course?**
Yes. [AISEOCourse.academy](https://aiseocourse.academy/) covers SEO fundamentals, AI search, GEO research, retrieval, entities, off-page authority, measurement and testing. Ahrefs, Semrush and HubSpot academies all have free AI-search or AEO courses with certificates. Verify access terms before you plan a schedule around them.

**What should an AI SEO course cover?**
At minimum: SEO fundamentals, AI-search fundamentals, GEO/AEO, retrieval, citation analysis, content, entities, off-page sources, measurement and testing. The detailed framework is in the [AI SEO Course guide](https://aiseocourse.co/).

**Do I need to learn traditional SEO first?**
Not years of it, but the fundamentals. AI-search optimisation still depends on sites and information that can be crawled, indexed, interpreted and trusted.

**Is GEO replacing SEO?**
No. GEO expands the visibility problem into generative experiences. It does not make technical SEO, content quality, authority or intent irrelevant.

**What's the difference between AI SEO and GEO?**
AI SEO is the umbrella, and vendors stretch it to include using AI tools in your workflow - a separate activity that makes courses look fuller. GEO specifically targets being retrieved and cited by generative models. Inspect the curriculum, not the label.

**What's the difference between GEO and AEO?**
GEO focuses on generative-engine visibility; AEO on being the extracted answer. They overlap heavily in practice.

**Are AI SEO certificates worth it?**
For formal learning or procurement signalling, sometimes. As proof of implementation skill, no. If you need one, the free HubSpot and Semrush certificates are the least painful.

**How much does an AI SEO course cost?**
On 30 September 2026 the verified span ran from $0 (Ahrefs, Semrush, HubSpot) to $1,995/year (Reforge). Live cohorts: $299 to $499. Communities: roughly $27 to $259 a month as listed, several unverified. Marketplace courses: list prices near $85, permanently discounted. Prices move - check the provider's page, not a roundup's screenshot.

**Is AI SEO Rainmakers a course?**
It is a paid community with training inside it: live weekly support, tests, tools, repositories, audits and an operator room. [Check the current listing](https://www.skool.com/ai-seo-rainmakers/about?ref=664c257fd6a84a3b94a2cb2a616d053d) *(affiliate link)*.

**Why is Rainmakers linked with an affiliate URL?**
Because the repository publisher may receive a commission if somebody joins through it. The relationship is disclosed so you can weigh the recommendation accordingly.

**Should I pay for AI SEO training before learning free material?**
Usually not. Learn enough free material to find your actual bottleneck, then pay for the thing that removes it.

**Can an AI SEO course guarantee citations?**
No responsible course can. AI answers and cited sources vary between runs and platforms. Use repeated measurements.

**Does schema make a site rank in AI answers?**
Structured data can help systems understand page information where appropriate. It is not a ranking switch for AI answers, whatever a sales page implies.

**Is `llms.txt` required for AI SEO?**
No single optional file is a strategy. Prioritise crawlability, indexability, useful information, entities, evidence, authority, source availability and measurement.

**Should AI SEO content be longer?**
Not automatically. Make it more useful, precise and evidenced. Length is an output of the information required, not the strategy.
