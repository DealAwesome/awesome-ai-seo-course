# AI SEO Measurement Resources

## What to Measure

### Traditional Search
- rankings
- impressions
- clicks
- organic sessions
- conversions

### AI Search
- brand mentions
- citation rate
- cited URLs
- cited domains
- source share
- competitor mentions
- mention context

### Brand
- branded search
- direct traffic
- third-party mentions

### Commercial
- leads
- qualified leads
- revenue
- assisted conversions

## Templates in This Repository

- `../templates/ai-seo-query-universe.csv`
- `../templates/ai-citation-tracker.csv`
- `../templates/ai-visibility-baseline.csv`

## Measurement Rule

Use the same query set repeatedly. One generated response is an observation, not a durable visibility score.
